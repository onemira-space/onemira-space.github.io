2. Australian Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) & State Recording Consent Note

Australian states and territories (including NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT and NT) enforce strict Surveillance Devices and Listening Devices laws requiring participant knowledge and consent before recording private conversations. Using C1 alone does not make an organisation Privacy Act 1988 compliant; local transcription on your Mac or PC avoids sending C1 audio to a vendor transcription cloud, while full compliance depends on lawful consent, workstation security, retention settings, and any external AI APIs you optionally connect (radio certification: US FCC ID 2BXIX-C1).
